# Army Creation

Create separate Blue and Red lists, with a nation/uniform scheme, name and agreed budget. Templates are original custom lists rather than assertions of historical organizations.

Each list has a company commander, 1–4 platoons with a PC and 1–4 rifle/SMG squads per platoon, up to three attached HMGs per platoon and up to three company HMGs. Choose troop quality and commander bonuses. Optional designated recon adds independent movement and recon on 5+. Smoke support supplies an FO and finite missions.

The builder shows fighting stands, total stands, points and the company break threshold. A custom army breaks at half its starting fighting squad/HMG stands, rounded up. The Farm capture objective also remains available. Lists over their chosen budget cannot be accepted until the force or budget is adjusted.

Displayed verified bonus-selection costs: Rifle Green 2.5 / Regular 3 / Veteran 4.5 per squad; SMG Green 2 / Regular 2.5; HMG 6 regardless of quality; PC +1 costs 2 and +2 costs 4; CC +1 costs 4 and +2 costs 6, plus 2 for Veteran quality. Recon costs +1 per designated rifle/SMG squad. Zero-bonus commanders remain unpriced, and Veteran SMG costing requires the explicit agreed value. The interface identifies incomplete totals. No historical period purchase limits or organization-specific command exceptions are inferred from these custom lists.

Smoke choices are 50–60 mm (2 stands, 12 missions, 4 points), 81–82 mm (3 stands, 12 missions, 6 points), and 120 mm (4 stands, 10 missions, 7 points). These are scenario allocations and full battery point costs; only smoke missions are provided in this infantry release. HE fire is outside the current module.

Export/import JSON army lists independently of game saves. Accepted lists are also retained locally for reuse. Historical lists and the complete Crossfire army catalog remain future work; the builder makes no claim to enforce those omitted systems.
